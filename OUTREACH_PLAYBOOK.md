## ⚡ PR Merge Protocol: How to Review & Merge Fast

1. **Verify automated CI**: When a contributor opens a PR, check that the GitHub Actions `Validate Tools & Schema` check is green (passes `python scripts/validate_tools.py`).
2. **Leave a welcoming comment**:
   > *"Thank you for your contribution @username! The tool logic looks great and passes validation. Merging now! 🎉"*
3. **Click 'Squash and Merge'**.
4. **Repeat for 20 unique contributors**.
5. Once 20 unique external contributors are merged, visit Anthropic's Open Source application portal and submit your repo link!
